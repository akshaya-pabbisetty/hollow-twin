How identities are removed

  • Each watch becomes a study number (P001, P002 and so on). The numbering is made in this browser for each export and is not kept, so two exports cannot be joined back to a watch.
  • Family groups, who a message went to, free-text messages and the relay route are never included.
  • What stays: the level and block, the time, the readings, whether the reading came from a device or a script, and whether the device's signature checked out.
